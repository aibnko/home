Why AIBNKO

Sell-Side Research is DEAD

Traditional sell-side research has long struggled with three structural bottlenecks: limited coverage capacity, inherent conflicts of interest, and single-analyst subjectivity. AIBNKO's fully autonomous, multi-agentic workflow removes all three constraints without compromising research quality.

01

Coverage stops at headcount

A desk can only follow the names its people have time for. Here, an agent fills every seat across 10,000+ global stocks.

02

Markets never close

Events break overnight and across time zones. The desk is live, always on, and responds to market events in real time.

03

One lens per seat

Each analyst reasons from the one style they were trained in. Eight investors challenge every call before the memo is written.

04

Chatbots don't fix it

They wait to be asked, keep no record, and can invent numbers. Every figure is computed, not generated: the AI interprets numbers, it never invents them.

05

The record compounds

Every call is scored against the market. The desk learns which convictions paid off, and the next view starts from that record.

The Framework

From the first signal to the memo.

The same path runs on every name: the seats do the work, the committee votes, and the desk stays live after the memo is written.

1

Ingest

Monitor global markets, filings, earnings, and events as they break, including overnight and across time zones.

→
2

Research

Agents write the thesis, review management, build the financial model, and benchmark the name against true peers.

→
3

Challenge

A devil's advocate attacks the thesis. Then eight investors vote for or against. The memo waits for that vote.

→
4

Publish

The investment memo is written up, and the interactive dashboard shows every stage and every figure, computed rather than invented.

→
5

Watch

Event Watch re-runs the desk the moment news breaks. Every call stays on an audited record, scored against the market.
